Italy

An European country with a long Mediterranean coastline, has left a powerful mark on Western culture and cuisine. Its capital, Rome, is home to the Vatican as well as landmark art and ancient ruins. Other major cities include Florence, with Renaissance masterpieces such as Michelangelo’s "David" and Brunelleschi's Duomo; Venice, the city of canals; and Milan, Italy’s fashion capital.

Palazzo Avino Hotel, Italy

Palazzo Avino Hotel
Overlooking a dramatic stretch of Amalfi coastline, luxury hotel Palazzo Avino sits high on a hillside that was once home to noble families. Standing here since the 12th century, the ‘Pink Palace’ (as the locals call it) might no longer be home to a Duca or Duchesse, but it still has Italian grandeur galore, with sumptuous suites, polished-walnut furniture and decorative tile floors.

Making the most of its views, the hotel has several tiers of verdant gardens and sun-trapping terraces – one of which is home to the hotel’s Michelin-starred restaurant Rossellinis, one of the most romantic places in town.

Hotel Pullman Timi Ama Sardegna, Italy

Hotel Pullman Timi Ama Sardegna
The Pullman Timi Ama hotel sits in a stunning location between a small, natural juniper grove, (overlooking a natural salt lagoon, where pink flamingos can be spotted in summer) and a wonderfully wide expanse of fine white sand bordering incredibly crystal clear waters.

The main restaurant is La Veranda, which has terrace facing the swimming pool and serves a wide selection of appetising dishes for a buffet-style breakfast and dinner, included for guests on half board. Restaurant I Ginepri is open from mid May until early October weather permitting.

It offers light meals á la carte for lunch and dinner (full menu prices applicable). Also open for lunch is the a la carte Restaurant Mediterraneo, which also offers ‘Mediterraneo by night’ late dining from 10pm until 1am (full menu price applicable).

Luxor Cairo Beach Resort, Italy

Luxor Cairo Beach Resort
A hotel vacation in Jesolo, at the Luxor & Cairo The Beach Resort, is a great time to visit magnificent Venice, to scout out the itineraries that cross the uncontaminated and enchanting nature of the Venice Lagoon or to play sports and have fun at Aqualandia, water sports, golf, tennis, go-karting.

Sail boats and catamarans, windsurfing and jet skiing: just a few steps away from the Luxor & Cairo The Beach Resort, rentals and schools guarantee a true beach vacation while those who can't stop playing their favorite sports even while staying at a hotel in Jesolo, tennis courts are near the hotel and the beautiful 18-hole golf course is just 2 Km away. For race fans, the famous Pista Azzurra go-kart track is only 3 km from the Luxor & Cairo The Beach Resort.
